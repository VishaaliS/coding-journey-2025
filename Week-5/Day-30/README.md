## The Idea: Redux

**Full name:** Re-diagnosis system for mental health disorders

### The Problem

**Medical diagnosis "freezing":**

Once a patient receives a diagnosis (especially mental health), it often follows them for life.

Even when:
- Symptoms change
- Patient recovers
- Condition evolves

The original diagnosis stays in medical records. Patients are treated based on outdated information.

### The Solution

**Redux: Periodic re-evaluation system**

- Evidence-based re-diagnosis criteria
- Patient symptom tracking over time
- Algorithm suggests when re-evaluation needed
- Prevents diagnosis from becoming permanent label

### Scope Decision

**Mental health disorders only:**
- Depression
- Anxiety
- PTSD
- Bipolar disorder

**Why this scope?**  
Smart constraint. Narrow enough to build in hackathon timeline. Broad enough to demonstrate value.
